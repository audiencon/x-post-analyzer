## Design Context

### Users
Indie founders, builders, and operators who already write on X. They open PostRoast in the last five minutes before posting. The job is not "generate content." The job is: tell me if this draft will die, then give me a version I would actually ship.

### Brand Personality
Honest, editorial, slightly mean, useful. Three words: sharp, paper, ink. The interface should feel like a red-pen workshop, not a growth dashboard. Confidence after the roast, not hype before it.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial critique studio. Warm charcoal surfaces (never pure black or pure white). One mark color — ink red — used only on the roast, scores that sting, and primary CTAs. Display serif (Newsreader) for headlines; Figtree for UI. Light-paper marketing moments are allowed; the tool stays dark-warm. Anti-references: Inter + X-blue + glow cards, generic AI dark mode, icon-above-heading feature grids, fake social proof.

### Design Principles
1. The roast is the product. Everything else is secondary.
2. Never show an empty ad. Unsold inventory looks like a dead product.
3. Instant value after a cheap sign-in. Ten free roasts a day. No credit card for the first pass.
4. Proof is a before/after, not a star rating.
5. Motion only on score reveal, rewrite swap, and card export — never decoration.
